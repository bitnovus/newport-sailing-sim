Harbor 20 · Public Beta

Experimental simulator. Not for navigation or formal sailing instruction. Use official charts and sound seamanship on the water.

Sailing the Harbor 20
  • ←/→ or A/D tiller (real mode: push right = turn left)
  • Tiller slider holds the selected helm angle · CENTER returns it to 0°
  • S/W mainsheet in/out  ·  C center tiller
  • Jib trim slider limits the self-tending club boom; wind swings it across automatically
  • SPACE drop MOB marker  ·  E electric drive
  • CLEAR remove the track and reset/remove the MOB marker
  • V chase/chartplotter  ·  M satellite/chart
  • Wheel / + / − zoom (works in both views)
  • Keep the boat out of the no-go zone (~35° off the wind) — watch the windex.

Privacy: your track stays in this browser. Map tiles and weather are requested directly from their providers, which receive normal web request data such as your IP address and referring site.

Data and services: OpenStreetMap · OpenSeaMap · Esri · Open-Meteo
